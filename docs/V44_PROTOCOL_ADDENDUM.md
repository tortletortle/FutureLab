# V0.44 provenance-only protocol addendum

The primary scientific comparison, seed, split, budgets, costs, methods, and evaluation in `docs/V43_PROTOCOL.md` are unchanged. No access to complete real outcomes has occurred in this development environment.

- The V43 compressed sklift input remains the pre-specified format and digest. A plain CSV is allowed as a separately labeled fallback, with SHA-256 `0e5893329d8b93cefecc571777672028290ab69865718020c78c7284f291aece`, reported by independent project documentation. Its equivalence to the original publisher bytes or the V43 compressed mirror has not been independently verified. A fallback evaluation is therefore non-confirmatory relative to the exact V43 protocol until the ordered records are compared. No re-sorting, deduplication, outcome transformation or test tuning is permitted.
- The published arm counts (21,306 no email, 21,307 men's merchandise email, 21,387 women's merchandise email), schema, finite nonnegative spend and domain checks are required in addition to integrity checks. Passing checks establishes consistency with documentation, not authenticity or license.
- The CSV mirrors are accepted research inputs only; do not redistribute source data or infer permission for commercial use.
- The manifest records the actually loaded bytes, package versions, protocol/benchmark hashes and execution status. An incomplete result directory is not evidence; check provenance status and logs. The workflow is manual dispatch only and uploads reports, not original data.
- Do not repeatedly rerun a real holdout to tune score selection. The workflow produces no claim simply by being dispatched.
